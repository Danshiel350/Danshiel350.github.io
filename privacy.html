Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

"Dan Shiel personal CLI" is a private tool used only by its owner, Dan Shiel. It accesses the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar through Google's APIs, with the owner's permission, to read, label and archive his own email and read his own calendar.

  • Only the owner's own Google account is accessed. The tool has no other users.
  • Data stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
